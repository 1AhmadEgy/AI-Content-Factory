AI CONTENT FACTORY — RETRY CONTRACT

Retry is a Job Engine policy, not a Worker decision.

RULES
- jobId is preserved.
- attempt increments for every execution attempt.
- each attempt receives a unique executionId.
- maxAttempts is explicit.
- only retryable/conditionally-retryable failures may retry.
- exponential backoff with jitter is recommended.
- cancellation overrides retry.
- license and invalid-input failures normally do not retry.
- hardware failures may retry only after routing/resource changes.
- every retry is audited and emits JOB_RETRYING.

RECOMMENDED FORMULA
backoff = min(maxBackoff, baseBackoff * 2^(attempt-1)) + jitter

A retry must not duplicate durable output or corrupt asset provenance.

ACCEPTANCE
[ ] attempt tracked [ ] executionId unique [ ] maxAttempts enforced [ ] backoff [ ] cancellation override [ ] audit [ ] tests
