AI CONTENT FACTORY — SCHEDULER CONTRACT

Scheduler decides when a queued job may execute.

INPUTS
job status, priority, availableAt, dependencies, worker capability, provider health, hardware state, resource limits, license state, concurrency limits.

RULES
1. Scheduler never bypasses dependencies.
2. Scheduler never executes BLOCKED jobs.
3. Scheduler respects cancellation.
4. Scheduler checks resource and capability constraints.
5. Scheduler must avoid duplicate active executions for non-concurrent jobs.
6. Scheduling decisions are observable.
7. Fairness must prevent low-priority starvation.
8. Retry backoff is enforced by availableAt.

OUTPUT
A scheduling decision is either dispatch, defer, block, cancel, or fail with a stable reason.

ACCEPTANCE
[ ] Dependency-aware [ ] Resource-aware [ ] Priority-aware [ ] Cancellation-aware [ ] Observable [ ] Scheduler tests
