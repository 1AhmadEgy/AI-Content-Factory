AI CONTENT FACTORY — JOB FAILURE & RECOVERY CONTRACT

PURPOSE
Define deterministic recovery for asynchronous AI jobs.

FAILURE CLASSES
Transient: network timeout, provider unavailable, temporary queue/resource pressure.
Permanent: invalid input, unsupported capability, blocked license, corrupted request.
Conditional: insufficient VRAM, model unavailable, provider degradation.

RECOVERY
1. Persist failure.
2. Classify error.
3. Check cancellation.
4. Check attempt/maxAttempts.
5. Apply backoff if retryable.
6. Re-check dependencies/resources/provider/license.
7. Retry same job with new executionId, or route to explicit fallback.
8. After exhaustion, mark FAILED and optionally send to DLQ.

BACKOFF
Use capped exponential backoff with jitter. Do not busy-loop.

CANCELLATION
Cancellation overrides pending retries. A cancelled execution must not be restarted by stale queue messages.

PAUSE/RESUME
Pause prevents new execution/progress commitment while preserving job state. Resume re-enters scheduling; it does not reset history.

DEAD LETTER
Jobs repeatedly failing after policy limits are retained with reason, attempts, last executionId and recovery metadata.

CIRCUIT BREAKER
Provider/worker health may temporarily prevent dispatch after repeated transient failures. Recovery occurs after cooldown and health checks.
