AI CONTENT FACTORY — QUEUE CONTRACT

Queue owns durable waiting work and delivery semantics.

CONTRACT
enqueue(jobId, priority, availableAt)
dequeue(workerCapabilities)
ack(jobId, executionId)
nack(jobId, executionId, reason)
requeue(jobId, availableAt)
inspect(jobId)

RULES
- Queue messages identify durable jobs; payloads must not become the source of truth.
- Delivery is at-least-once unless a stronger guarantee is explicitly implemented.
- Consumers must be idempotent.
- Priority and fairness are deterministic.
- Acknowledgement occurs only after the execution state is safely persisted.
- Poison jobs are routed to dead-letter handling.
- Queue outage must not corrupt job state.
